This week's plan

Day 1 — Strength A

2 sets • controlled pace

🧘 Daily Mobility (5 min)

Do this most days — including rest days

Cat-Cow, thoracic rotation, doorway stretch and child's pose — gentle movement for your mid/upper back around the L4–L5 disc bulge. On the rotation, only go as far left as feels free — don't push into the strain. Should feel easing, never sharp or worse afterward.

🎯 Today's focus

  • Move with control, not speed.
  • Keep your core gently engaged.
  • Breathe steadily.
  • Stop if you feel pain.

💡 For you

Your goal isn't to return to your old 49 kg. It's to build enough relative strength — legs, arms, core and back — to confidently control your current body weight through jumping, landing and diving.

🔎

🍽️ Eating for recovery & strength

Support muscle repair, joints and steady energy

This isn't a weight-loss diet — it's built to fuel rebuilding strength and recovering from your L4–L5 disc bulge and the new upper-spine/shoulder pain. Rough starting targets for your current weight (~68 kg):

110–150gProtein / day
3–4LWater / day
7–9hSleep (tissue repair)

Protein target is roughly 1.6–2.2 g per kg of bodyweight — the standard range for rebuilding strength after a long inactive stretch. Adjust with a dietitian if you have any digestive, kidney or other condition that changes this.

🍽️ Build your plate

Same simple structure each meal
Protein — palm-sized Vegetables — half the plate Carbs — cupped hand Fat — thumb-sized

Bigger carb portion around training days (rice, oats, potatoes, fruit) to fuel the workout and help recovery; slightly smaller on rest days.

🌿 For your back & joints

Foods that support recovery, not just muscle
Lean in on
Oily fish (salmon, sardines, mackerel) or a fish-oil/flaxseed source for omega-3s, colourful vegetables and fruit for antioxidants, nuts, olive oil, turmeric/ginger if you enjoy them, and enough calcium + vitamin D (dairy, fortified foods, sensible sun exposure) for bone and disc health.
Go easy on
Ultra-processed and fried food, excess added sugar, and heavy alcohol — all linked with more systemic inflammation, which won't help a healing back.

📋 Sample day

Adjust portions and swap freely for your taste/budget
Breakfast
Eggs or Greek yoghurt with oats and fruit, or paneer/tofu with whole-grain toast.
Lunch
Dal/legumes or grilled chicken/fish + rice or roti + a big portion of vegetables.
Pre/post-workout
Banana + a handful of nuts before; a protein source + carb (e.g. yoghurt + fruit, or a protein shake + toast) within a couple hours after.
Dinner
Fish/chicken/paneer/legumes + vegetables + a moderate carb portion, lighter than lunch if training is done for the day.

This is general guidance, not a personalised medical or dietetic plan — a registered dietitian can tailor this if you have specific conditions, allergies, or aren't seeing progress.

🚑 Seek urgent care if...

Don't treat these as ordinary posture pain
  • 🔴Chest pain alongside the back pain
  • 🔴Significant difficulty breathing
  • 🔴Fainting or feeling like you might faint
  • 🔴Weakness or numbness in an arm or leg
  • 🔴Pain spreading into your arm or jaw

🩺 Get a physio assessment before...

Especially with L4–L5 disc bulge + new upper-spine/shoulder pain
1Progressing to Phase 3 (jumping, cutting, diving drills).
2Returning to full-contact or competitive football.
3If pain in the Monitor tab stays elevated or keeps climbing over several check-ins.
4If any exercise reliably reproduces sharp or radiating pain, rather than normal muscle fatigue.

A physio can look at your spine, posture, and hip/core strength directly and adjust this program — something no app can safely do from a description alone. Tell them specifically that turning your head to the left strains the area but rotation itself is otherwise free — that detail helps narrow down what's going on.

✅ Training principles

Keep these in mind every session
🐢 Progress slowly. You're rebuilding years of deconditioning — consistency beats intensity.
🧍 Prioritise control and form over reps or speed, especially near your spine.
🛑 Sharp, sudden or radiating pain means stop, not push through.
↩️ Don't force left neck/shoulder rotation past where it's comfortable — work in the pain-free range and let a physio address the restriction directly.
🪑 Break up long sitting at your desk — stand, walk or stretch every 30–45 min.
😴 Sleep and protein are when the actual rebuilding happens — they matter as much as the workouts.
